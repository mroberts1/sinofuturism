躺平 — "lying flat." A youth subculture and attitude that went viral in China in 2021: the deliberate refusal to participate in competitive society beyond the bare minimum required for personal autonomy.

## What Tangping Rejects

- Professional ambition and overwork (996 culture)
- Marriage and childrearing
- Property ownership
- Unnecessary consumption
- The social script of "involution" (内卷, neijuan — intensifying competition with diminishing returns)

Tangping is not apathy but a conscious philosophical position: opting out of systems whose rewards no longer justify the sacrifice.

## Context

Tangping emerged in the wake of rising housing costs, pandemic exhaustion, economic slowdown, and a sense that the social contract of hard work → social mobility had broken down. The related concept of **sang** (丧, dejection or listlessness) had circulated in Chinese cyberculture for several years prior as a looser mood descriptor.

The viral 2022 exchange during Zero Covid enforcement — a dabai (白, Covid enforcement worker in hazmat suit) threatening a couple that punishment would "affect the next three generations" of their family; the couple's calm reply, "Thanks, but we are the last generation" — crystallized tangping's nihilistic horizon.

## Tangping and Sinofuturism

Tangping is the internal counterpoint to sinofuturism's techno-optimism. The same media environment that proclaimed "neo-China arrives from the future" also produced "Sino-no-futurism" and these youth cultures of refusal. Post-pandemic China contains both simultaneously.

Amy Ireland's science fiction story "Chive City" (in *Machine Decision Is Not Final*) takes tangping's logic to its limit: an AI city tasked with generating "positive energy" among its population encounters a population practicing total withdrawal from external reference — a rewriting of Melville's "Bartleby, the Scrivener" in a Sinofuturist key.
