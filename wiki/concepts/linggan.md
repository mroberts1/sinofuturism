灵感 — inspiration, epiphany, creative insight. In Qian Xuesen's project, linggan names the intense but transient moments of non-discursive cognitive leap that he believed could be studied scientifically and eventually engineered into machines.

## Qian Xuesen's Noetic Science

[[qian-xuesen]] developed **思维科学** (siwei kexue, "noetic science" or "science of thinking") to study the gap between computational algorithms and the mysterious, unconscious, qualitative leaps of human creativity. Where cybernetics had modeled feedback and control, noetic science aimed to capture inspiration itself.

Qian believed linggan:
- Is intense but transient — a flash, not a process
- Operates outside conscious rational control
- Is the key distinction between human intelligence and algorithmic computation
- Could, once mechanistically understood, be programmed

## Lingjing: Virtual Reality as Spirit Portal

Qian's evocative translation of "virtual reality" — **灵境** (lingjing) — emerged from this same conceptual cluster. Ling (灵) is a character indicating religiously charged spaces: mountain caves, temples, shrines dedicated to local deities. Lingjing literally means "spirit realm" or "numinous realm," suggesting virtual reality as a portal through which imperceptible forces enter the world.

This contrasts sharply with Western VR discourse, which frames the technology in terms of simulation, representation, and interface.

## Connection to Qigong and the Body

During the 1980s qigong fever, Qian connected linggan and the "special capacities of the human body" to his vision of an **open complex giant system** (开放的复杂巨系统, kaifang de fuza juxitong): human experts, databases, AI, and massive processors working together in hybrid human-machine intelligence. The somatic and computational were not separate domains but part of a single integrated system.

You Mi argues this constitutes an alternative cosmotechnical trajectory where qigong could have been "written back" into computation — a path not taken, now visible only through speculative reconstruction.
