The organizing contrast of Han's *Shanzhai* and a structuring opposition that runs through the sinofuturism KB's engagement with Chinese philosophy more broadly. Western metaphysics is founded on **Being** (*Sein*, *ousia*): what is permanent, self-identical, underlying all change. Chinese thought is founded on **process**: the Tao embraces change where essence resists it.

## The Contrast

| Western (Being) | Chinese (Process) |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Immutable essence | Continuous transformation |
| *Creatio ex nihilo* | [[decreation]] — no absolute beginning |
| Original as unique event | Origin as retrospective construction |
| Fixed identity | Sliding weight (*quan*) |
| Ruins as monuments to rupture | No ruins — no founding events to mourn |
| IP/copyright as fixed right | IP as situational power |

## The Pragmatism of Process

Han describes Chinese thought as *pragmatic* in a specific sense: it traces not essences but the changeable constellations of things (*pragmata*). The task is to recognize the course of things and correlate situationally with it — not to reveal an underlying truth. Power (*quan li*) belongs not to subjectivity but to situativity.

This is what Hegel misread as immorality: in the absence of fixed essences, commitments look provisional. But provisional is not arbitrary — *quan* always seeks balance, not chaos.

## Relation to Cosmotechnics

Process vs Being is the ontological dimension of [[cosmotechnics]]. [[yuk-hui]]'s framework argues Western technology is grounded in Gestell (enframing, world as standing reserve) — which is exactly the Being-logic: the world as a fixed resource awaiting exploitation. Chinese *qi* (tools) + Dao (Way) proposes technology as the unification of cosmic order and moral order through process — never extracting a standing reserve but moving with transformation.

## Relation to the AI Debate

Bratton's analysis of [[rengong-zhineng]] converges here: Western AI locates intelligence in individual consciousness (a Being-logic — the stable, self-identical subject). Chinese *rengong* locates it in coordinated production, collective process. [[alphago]] does not "think" in the Western individualist sense; it plays by process logic — minimizing loss, tracking constellations, never positing a final form.
