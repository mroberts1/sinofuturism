Byung-Chul Han's term (*Ent-schöpfung* in German) for the desubstantializing operation at the heart of Chinese thought. Decreation empties out Being — not by destroying it but by dissolving its claim to essence, immutability, and self-identity. What remains is trace, process, and emptiness.

Decreation stands opposed to *creatio ex nihilo* — the Western model of creation as a unique originating act that produces something wholly new from nothing. Where Western creation posits a beginning in the emphatic sense, decreation refuses any absolute beginning or end. The Tao, as "the way," is its figure: a path that is flat and continually changing course, not deepening toward a mystery.

## Shanzhai is Decreation

Han's central claim: **[[shanzhai]] is decreation**. The shanzhai movement opposes identity with transformational difference, Being with process, essence with path. Labels mutate (Adidas → Adidos → Adadas → Adadis) in a Dadaist game that parodies economic monopoly while enacting a genuinely different ontology of originality.

This reframes [[shanzhai]] definitively: it is not a failure of originality or a cultural deficiency but the contemporary expression of a Chinese philosophical tradition that never posited *creatio ex nihilo* in the first place. Copyright law — built on Western metaphysics of Being — simply applies the wrong ontology.

Han distinguishes decreation from the Western notion of ruins. Ruins presuppose an identity founded on a unique event — a rupture, a loss to be preserved. Chinese thought has no ruins because it knows no founding events; there is nothing to memorialize, only process to continue.

## Decreation and AI

The decreation framework recontextualizes several AI debates in the sinofuturism KB:

- **Deep learning** as a decreative process: models do not create *ex nihilo* but absorb, decompose, and reassemble existing material — transformational variation over original invention
- **[[alphago]]'s "God Move"**: Lee Sedol's counter-move was made possible by the new terrain AlphaGo opened. Neither move was creation from nothing; both were responses within a field of traces
- **[[deepseek]]** as decreative engineering: taking existing architectures and making them work differently, cheaper, more efficiently — the sliding weight of *quan* applied to compute
