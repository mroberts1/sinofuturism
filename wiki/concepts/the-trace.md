The Chinese concept of *zhen ji* (真跡) — literally "authentic trace" — is Han's key for dismantling the Western concept of the original. The trace is not a copy of a prior presence, nor a sign pointing toward a stable referent. It *differs from itself*: it keeps the artwork perpetually open, preventing it from achieving a final form or condensing into a monomorphic presence.

## The Structure of the Trace

Han draws a parallel to Freud's memory-traces: not stable representations of experience but products of complex construction subject to continual rearrangement. New constellations alter earlier traces; past, present, and future interfuse. The Chinese original shares this structure — not a unique creation event but an *unending process of rearrangement*.

The trace **effaces artistic subjectivity**: no creator's presence underwrites the work's authenticity. Instead, the work opens to collective inscription — connoisseurs' seals, collectors' commentaries, the poetry of friends at farewell gatherings. The work is a palimpsest.

The trace **deconstructs originality**: if the original is always already a trace — processual, differential, retrospective — then the distinction between original and copy collapses. The "original" is what a given era constructs from the traces available.

## Chang Dai-Chien

The exemplary case: Chang Dai-chien, the twentieth century's most celebrated Chinese painter, whose career was built on forgeries of Old Masters — including "forgeries" of lost works known only from written descriptions, creating originals that had never existed. For Han, these are *more* original than originals: they demonstrate that the origin was never a fixed point but always a retrospective construction. Han's conclusion: *the greater a master, the emptier his oeuvre.*

## Relation to Shanzhai

The trace is the deep logic beneath [[shanzhai]]. When a shanzhai phone adds features the original lacks, it is not failing to copy but enacting the trace-logic: absorbing, rearranging, and extending. The "original" Nokia or Samsung is itself a trace in a longer sequence — shanzhai makes this visible.

## Relation to AI

Large language models operate as trace-machines: they do not retrieve stored originals but reconstruct plausible outputs from statistical relationships among traces of human text. Han's framework suggests this is not a deficiency (the model "doesn't really understand") but a particular mode of intelligence — one that Chinese thought is better equipped to theorize than Western metaphysics of Being.

The [[dark-forest-theory-of-intelligence]] also rhymes with the trace: an intelligence that does not announce itself, leaves no stable signature, and operates through indirection — tracial rather than authorial.
