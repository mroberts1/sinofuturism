## Key Questions in the Literature

- Is there a distinctly Chinese technology ontology ([[cosmotechnics]])? Or does the cosmological framing remain rhetorical?
- How does [[rengong-zhineng]]'s labor-embodied connotations reshape what AI is asked to do?
- Can [[qian-xuesen]]'s path — integrating somatic science, qigong, and computation — be retrospectively operationalized?
- Does the [[dark-forest-theory-of-intelligence]] imply that AI alignment is structurally impossible?
- What is the relationship between [[shanzhai]] innovation and genuine technical divergence?
- Is [[tangping]] a temporary mood or a structural shift in Chinese political subjectivity?
