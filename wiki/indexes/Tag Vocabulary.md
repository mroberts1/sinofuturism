## Rules

- Tags derive from the field's own conceptual vocabulary — not generic terms like `#technology` or `#culture`
- Hyphenated, lowercase: `#sino-futurism` not `#SinoFuturism` or `#sino_futurism`
- Cross-cutting concepts get their own tag only if they recur across multiple sources
- One tag per major concept cluster; don't over-tag
